The same map played on the venue model: every label lands on its screen.

  • Every screen is built at its own pixel size.IMAG walls are 1216 × 592, the ceiling sunrays are as thin as 1536 × 128. We build to those exact sizes, so nothing is stretched.
  • What's outside a rectangle is never seen.The black areas of the frame don't exist in the room. That's why a full-screen video made for a TV can't simply be played on the walls: it has to be mapped.
  • Screens that meet line up.Where the walls meet, we align content row for row, so a move that crosses from one screen to the next continues cleanly across the join.
  • Two audiences.The stage walls, curves and ceiling face the dance floor. The outdoor towers and arch face the pool: a second audience that sees different screens.

03 · Transparent logo vs background

Every screen plays two layers.

Think of each screen as a stack. Underneath is the background: moving visuals that fill every pixel. On top sits your logo, with nothing around it, so the background keeps moving behind your mark.

Background · the look

Full-frame visuals, edge to edge. The club's own library, mixed live by our visual operator, or a look designed for your event.

Logo · the overlay

Only your mark. Everywhere around it is transparent, so it floats over whatever plays beneath. Static, or animated with a transparent background.

Logo layer: transparent around the mark Background layer: visuals fill the screen
03 · The overlay file

What a transparent overlay looks like.

An overlay is the same 3840 × 2160 frame, with only the logos in it. The gold outlines are the screens; the checkerboard is transparent, so the room's visuals keep playing there.

  • One file per logoHealth Connect Partners, October 13, 2026: two overlays, one for each logo, swapped during the night.
  • Fitted inside every screenEach logo is scaled to sit whole inside its rectangle, with space around it, never cropped.
  • Only on the logo screensIMAG SR and SL, Center, the DJ booth, the outdoor towers and the arch. Everything else stays transparent.
  • Played over the backgroundOur operator layers it on top of the visuals, so the room keeps moving and your logo stays put.

03 · Placement

Where your logo goes.

  • Logo screensIMAG SR and SL, Center, the DJ booth, the two outdoor towers and the arch. The ceiling rays can carry a logo too.
  • Always the whole markA logo is scaled to fit inside each screen, never cropped. A 15:1 ceiling ray and a portrait tower need different shapes of logo, so send both a wide and a stacked version.
  • The ceiling repeatsEach sunray row plays on two opposite rays, so a logo on a ray appears twice.
  • The rest keeps movingThe curves carry visuals, not logos, which keeps the room alive around your brand.
03 · Your files

What to send us.

Most delays come from the logo file. Get these right and there's nothing else for your designer to produce.

Send
  • A PNG with a transparent background, or the vector original (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF).
  • A wide and a stacked version of the logo, so it fits long ceiling rays and portrait towers alike.
  • A version that reads on dark: a white or light mark. The room is dark and the screens are bright.
  • The largest file you have, plus brand colours (HEX or Pantone) if you have them.
Avoid
  • A JPG. JPGs can't be transparent, so the logo arrives with a box around it.
  • A logo on a white or coloured background, a screenshot, or a logo pasted into a slide.
  • A small web image. Our walls are larger than most screens your logo has been on.
  • Video at the wrong size. Video for the walls is built to the pixel map, 3840 × 2160.
